SECURITY BY DESIGN

Your machine. Your rules.

The product is designed around explicit authorization and bounded execution, not unrestricted remote administration.

✓
Workspace AllowlistAgents only access explicitly authorized project directories.
✓
Approval RequiredSensitive actions can pause for explicit user approval.
×
No Remote DesktopNo desktop video stream or mouse-and-keyboard takeover.
×
No Unrestricted Remote ShellCommands stay scoped to agent workflows and policy enforcement.
◆
Secrets Stay LocalProvider credentials stay on the workstation wherever possible.
◆
Auditable SessionsSessions, approvals and execution events can be traced.
